Scamming
What is scamming?

Scamming is when someone tricks you into giving away money, personal information, passwords, or access to your accounts. Scammers often use fake messages, emails, websites, prizes, or social media accounts to deceive people.

Warning signs!
      Someone is rushing you.
      You are promised something unbelievable.
      They ask for passwords or codes.
      There is a suspicious link.
      They ask for money unexpectedly.

How to protect yourself?

  1. 🔍 CHECK — Is the message really from who it claims to be?
  2. 🤔 THINK — Does the offer seem too good to be true?
  3. 🔗 DON’T CLICK — Never click suspicious links.
  4. 🔒 PROTECT — Never share passwords or personal information.
